# Audio asset provenance

- `SFX/royal-applause.wav`: edited from `Applause ii.ogg`, an authentic
  medium-auditorium audience recording by thore, released into the public
  domain. Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Applause_ii.ogg
- `Music/palace-loop.wav`: locally generated from eight original movements with
  Meta MusicGen Small, then equal-power crossfaded and circularly mixed into a
  189.52-second loop.
- `SFX/victory-fanfare.wav`: edited from `castlefanfare.ogg`, "Trumpet
  Fanfare" by gchoc (Graciano Choc / Bz.gamedev), licensed CC0. Source:
  https://opengameart.org/content/trumpet-fanfare
- `Voice/wow.wav`, `Voice/fabulous.wav`, and `Voice/victory-wow.wav`: locally
  synthesized with Kokoro using the `ef_dora` Spanish voice.

The MusicGen model license must be reviewed before commercial distribution.
